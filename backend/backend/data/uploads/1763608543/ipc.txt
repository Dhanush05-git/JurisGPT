Section 420 – Cheating and Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property
Whoever cheats and dishonestly induces any person to deliver property shall be punished with imprisonment up to seven years and fine.

Section 375 – Rape (Definition)
A man is said to commit rape if he has sexual intercourse with a woman under circumstances involving coercion, lack of consent, intoxication, or when she is under eighteen years of age.

Section 378 – Theft (Definition)
Whoever, intending to take dishonestly any movable property out of the possession of any person without that person's consent, commits theft.

Section 120B – Criminal Conspiracy
When two or more persons agree to do an illegal act, it amounts to criminal conspiracy. Punishment depends on the severity of the offence.
