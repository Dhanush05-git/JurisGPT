BNS Section 303 – Cheating
Whoever cheats any person and dishonestly induces delivery of property shall be punished with imprisonment which may extend to seven years or fine.

BNS Section 63 – Theft
A person commits theft if they unlawfully take movable property out of someone's possession with dishonest intent.

BNS Section 63A – Aggravated Theft
If theft involves house-breaking or use of force, punishment may extend to ten years.

BNS Section 94 – Criminal Conspiracy
An agreement by two or more persons to commit an illegal act constitutes criminal conspiracy under this section.
