Section 303 – Cheating Whoever cheats any person and dishonestly induces delivery of property shall be punished with imprisonment which may extend to seven years or fine.  BNS
Section 63 – Theft A person commits theft if they unlawfully take movable property out of someone's possession with dishonest intent.  BNS
Section 63A – Aggravated Theft If theft involves house-breaking or use of force, punishment may extend to ten years.  BNS
Section 94 – Criminal Conspiracy An agreement by two or more persons to commit an illegal act constitutes criminal conspiracy under this section.
Article 12 – Definition of State The term “State” includes the Government of India, Parliament, State Governments, State Legislatures, and all local or other authorities within the territory of India or under the control of the Government of India.
Article 14 – Equality before Law The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or equal protection of the laws within the territory of India.
Article 19 – Protection of Certain Rights All citizens shall have the right to freedom of speech, expression, assembly, association, movement, residence, and profession. These rights may be subject to reasonable restrictions in the interests of sovereignty, security, and public order.
Article 21 – Protection of Life and Personal Liberty No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.
Article 32 – Right to Constitutional Remedies The Supreme Court shall have the power to issue directions or orders for the enforcement of fundamental rights.
Section 420 – Cheating and Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property Whoever cheats and dishonestly induces any person to deliver property shall be punished with imprisonment up to seven years and fine.
Section 375 – Rape (Definition) A man is said to commit rape if he has sexual intercourse with a woman under circumstances involving coercion, lack of consent, intoxication, or when she is under eighteen years of age.
Section 378 – Theft (Definition) Whoever, intending to take dishonestly any movable property out of the possession of any person without that person's consent, commits theft.
Section 120B – Criminal Conspiracy When two or more persons agree to do an illegal act, it amounts to criminal conspiracy. Punishment depends on the severity of the offence.
